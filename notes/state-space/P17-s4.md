## Main idea and method

Efficiently model long input dependencies with the Structured State Space model S4.

Structured state matrices and efficient kernels make SSM training practical.

## Source-reported evidence

Evaluates long sequence modeling such as Long Range Arena and audio and language.

Evidence location: SSM formulation and long sequence experiments.

## Connection to this project — interpretation

Helps distinguish state-based mathematics/training from the chain rule itself.

## Limitations when applying the result

General sequence-modeling research, not evidence of predicting future streaming speech.

## Main idea and method

Original Common Voice paper describes a multilingual corpus built through community recording and validation.

Describes sentence recording, multi-contributor validation and construction of a public corpus.

The original paper's date/language/hour statistics and 2026 v27 release statistics describe different versions.

## Connection to this project — interpretation

A path toward accent/language diversity; first specify language, validated subset and speaker split.

## Limitations when applying the result

42,593 time is the total amount of recording of the entire v27 scripted language. Do not use validated English learning time or spontaneous conversation time.

## Main idea and method

A 10,000-hour multi-domain English ASR corpus from audiobooks, podcasts and YouTube.

Provides collection, transcription, alignment, filtering and subsets of 10/250/1,000/2,500/10,000 hours.

## Source-reported evidence

Provides multiple ASR baselines and evaluates cross-domain extensions.

Evidence location: Corpus description and official repository download information.

## Connection to this project — interpretation

This is follow-up candidate data to check whether the method identified in LibriSpeech is more natural and is maintained in a variety of voices.

## Limitations when applying the result

The official distribution requires an application. This project did not submit an application, accept a contract or download the audio; access conditions are recorded in the registry.
